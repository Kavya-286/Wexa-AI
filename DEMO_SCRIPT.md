## 3. Career recommendations
Open the Career Explorer page and highlight the multi-hop traversal from skills to related skills to jobs to companies. Explain the matching percentages and the path explanation.

Open Job Details and click a role such as AI Engineer. Show the required skill list, salary, and employer information.

## 8. Multi-hop graph relationship

Share the important Cypher example used by the backend. Explain how the application uses parameterized queries to traverse the graph safely and efficiently.
